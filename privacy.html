Operational Security

1. DATA COLLECTION: We do not log biological signatures or personal identifying information from casual reconnaissance of this site. Your coordinates and navigation paths remain local to your hardware.

2. INTEL USAGE: All geographic and meteorological data provided is for expedition planning only. Users are responsible for their own safety protocols in the field.

3. THIRD-PARTY ARCHIVES: Links to external data streams (e.g., Instagram) are outside our encryption zone. Review their protocols before transmitting data.
