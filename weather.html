REGIONAL METEOROLOGY REPORTS

01. Casaroro Falls

Status: Misty / Damp

High spray-induced humidity. Rocks are perpetually wet. Rain likelihood: 65% after 2PM.

02. Pulangbato Falls

Status: Clear / River-Cool

Lower valley winds. Moderate humidity. Ideal visibility for photography throughout the day.

03. Red Rock Hot Spring

Status: Steam Haze

Ambient temp raised by water activity. Feels 2°C warmer than surrounding forest.

04. Forest Camp

Status: Canopy Shaded

Direct sun blocked by trees. Temperatures remain steady at 22-24°C even at noon.

05. Tierra Alta

Status: High Wind Exposure

Unobstructed coastal winds. Light jackets recommended for sunset viewing.

06. Tejero Highland

Status: Sunny / Open

High UV exposure. Limited tree cover near slides. Peak heat between 11AM and 2PM.

07. Cata-al Museum

Status: Dry / Shielded

Indoor conditions. Perfect for escaping sudden highland downpours.

08. Japanese Shrine

Status: Brisk Mountain Air

Thinning air. Clouds often pass through the site during the monsoon season.

09. Valencia Market

Status: Urban Heat

Paved surfaces retain heat. 3°C warmer than higher altitude forest locations.

10. Mount Talinis

Status: UNPREDICTABLE

Sudden fog banks and temp drops to 14°C at night. Crucial: Check for storm signals.

11. The Barn

Status: Mild / Garden

Balanced microclimate. Protected from high winds by mango trees.

12. Mag-aso Vents

Status: Thermal Heat / Sulfurous

Heat radiating from earth. Air can feel heavy/acrid depending on wind direction.
