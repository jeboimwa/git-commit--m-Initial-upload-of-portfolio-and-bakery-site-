Quick Support
💬 Message us on WhatsApp
Do you do custom orders? ▼
Yes! We love custom orders. Contact us at least 3 days in advance for custom cakes and special requests. We'll work with you to bring your vision to life.
What's your delivery area? ▼
We deliver to Palinpinon, Valencia, and nearby areas. For locations outside our usual area, reach out via WhatsApp and we'll check if we can arrange delivery.
How much is delivery? ▼
Delivery is FREE on orders of ₱3,000 or more! Orders under ₱3,000 have a flat ₱150 delivery fee. Same-day orders must be placed before noon.
Do you have allergen info? ▼
Most of our items contain nuts, dairy, eggs, and/or gluten. If you have allergies, please mention them in the special instructions or message us directly.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept GCash, Maya, bank transfers, and Cash on Delivery (COD). Payment is due before delivery for online transfers.
How long can I store your baked goods? ▼
Fresh pastries are best enjoyed the same day. Breads stay fresh for 2-3 days in an airtight container. Cakes can be refrigerated for up to 5 days.
