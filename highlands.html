THE HIGHLANDS

LOC: 9.2819° N, 123.2450° E | ELEV: 300M - 1903M

Geographic Profile

Valencia, known as the "Highland Capital" of Negros Oriental, serves as the primary cool-climate zone of the province. Nestled against the eastern slopes of the Cuernos de Negros (Mount Talinis) volcano, the terrain is characterized by steep ravines, ancient fern forests, and a network of river systems fueled by geothermal heat.

Climate Variance

Unlike the coastal heat of Dumaguete, the Highlands maintain a consistent temperature drop of 3°C to 7°C. The "Little Baguio" microclimate is caused by orographic lift, where moisture from the Bohol Sea is forced up the mountain slopes, resulting in frequent afternoon mists and high humidity—ideal conditions for the lush vegetation found in Forest Camp and Casaroro.

Strategic Importance

This region acts as the "Water Tower" for the surrounding municipalities. The geothermal activity underneath the surface powers one of the largest renewable energy plants in the Philippines (EDC), while the surface-level sulfur vents like Mag-aso provide critical geological data for volcanic monitoring.
