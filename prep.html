Transport Logistics

  • Public Jeepney (Dumaguete Terminal) Located near the Public Market/Fruit Stands. Direct to Valencia Plaza. ₱20.00 / seat
  • Habal-Habal (Motorcycle Taxi) The fastest way to reach Casaroro or Pulangbato. Negotiate before boarding. ₱50.00 - ₱150.00 per trip
  • Full Day Extraction (Private) Hire a driver for 8 hours to visit all 12 locations in your own sequence. ₱800.00 - ₱1,200.00

Mission Fuel (Food)

  • Valencia Budbud (Sticky Rice) The primary high-carb fuel for hikers. Best bought at the Sunday Market. ₱15.00 - ₱25.00
  • Sikwate (Hot Chocolate) Local cacao drink. Highly recommended for cold mornings at the Japanese Shrine. ₱30.00 / cup
  • Standard Highland Meal Roasted chicken or "Lantaw" style dining at local eateries. ₱150.00 - ₱300.00

Required Loadout

  • Primary Footwear Closed-toe sandals with aggressive tread. Basalt rocks are incredibly slick.
  • Comms & Power 10,000mAh Powerbank. 4G signal is intermittent in deep ravines (Casaroro).
  • Hydration System Minimum 1.5L capacity. Mountain springs are beautiful but may cause stomach distress.
