Critical Safety Directives - All Sectors

CRITICAL

01. Casaroro Falls

  • Staircase Integrity: 350+ steps. Descend slowly; moisture makes steel treads extremely slick.
  • Flash Flood Zone: The ravine is narrow. If water turns muddy, evacuate the canyon floor immediately.
  • Hydraulic Force: Avoid the splash zone. The downward wind can cause disorientation.
MODERATE

02. Pulangbato Falls

  • Mineral Staining: High sulfur/iron content. Do not wear white; water will cause permanent orange stains.
  • Basin Depth: Variable depths in the red pools. Check depth with a stick before jumping.
  • Rock Grip: The red rocks are smoother than standard basalt; move with care.
LOW

03. Red Rock Hot Spring

  • Thermal Shock: Water is 38°C+. Limit soak time to 20 mins to prevent dizziness.
  • Hydration: Drink 500ml of water for every 30 mins of soaking to avoid dehydration.
  • Slippery Tiles: The moss around the thermal pipes is high-risk for falls.
MODERATE

04. Forest Camp

  • Child Safety: Multiple pool levels. Constant supervision is required for non-swimmers.
  • Hanging Bridges: Max capacity is 3 personnel. Do not sway or jump on the suspension lines.
  • Adventure Gear: Harnesses for ziplining must be double-checked by camp staff.
LOW

05. Tierra Alta

  • Wind Shear: Lighthouse deck is exposed. Secure hats and light equipment against high gusts.
  • Sun Exposure: The white Greek architecture reflects UV rays. High-SPF sunscreen required.
  • Stair Safety: Lighthouse spiral stairs are narrow; yield to ascending personnel.
MODERATE

06. Tejero Highland Park

  • ATV Ops: Helmets and closed shoes are non-negotiable. Maintain 10m spacing on trails.
  • Water Slides: Follow "Arms Crossed" protocol to prevent spinal or limb impact.
  • River Flow: River currents can increase after rain; check safety flags before entry.
LOW

07. Cata-al WWII Museum

  • UXO Safety: Some artifacts are unexploded ordnance (inert). Do not shake or drop heavy metal relics.
  • Rust/Tetanus: Relics are aged iron. Avoid contact with skin if you have open wounds.
  • Fragile Intel: Do not use flash photography on paper documents or uniforms.
MODERATE

08. Japanese Shrine

  • Altitude Prep: High elevation means thinner air. Pace your ascent to avoid "mountain sickness."
  • Isolated Area: No cellular signal. Carry a whistle or signaling device for emergencies.
  • Trail Debris: Heavy leaf litter hides loose rocks. Watch your footing.
MODERATE

09. Valencia Market

  • Traffic Intel: Habal-habals move fast. Stand 1m back from the main road.
  • Security: Keep wallets/comms in front pockets. Sunday crowds increase pickpocket risk.
  • Food Hygiene: Only consume fully cooked "Budbud." Avoid tap water in the market zone.
CRITICAL

10. Mount Talinis

  • Guide Mandatory: Do not attempt Apolong or Bediao trails without a licensed guide.
  • Hypothermia: Temps drop to 14°C at Lake Nailig. Thermal blankets are mandatory.
  • Limatik (Leeches): Use alcohol spray or leech socks in the "Green Gate" jungle sections.
LOW

11. The Barn

  • Fire Hazard: Rustic wood structure. Strictly no smoking near the main barn walls.
  • Floor Levels: Steps are uneven to maintain aesthetic. Watch for trip hazards in low light.
  • Insect Alert: Open-air dining. Apply repellent during "mosquito hour" (sunset).
CRITICAL

12. Mag-aso Sulfur Vents

  • Gas Inhalation: Hydrogen Sulfide ($H_{2}S$) is present. If you smell rotten eggs, move upwind.
  • Ground Heat: Steam vents can hit 100°C. Do not touch wet ground near active vents.
  • Respiratory: Personnel with asthma must carry inhalers or avoid the direct vent zone.
